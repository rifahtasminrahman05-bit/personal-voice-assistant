Octo is a small language model that lives inside this assistant.
It reads text one character at a time and learns what comes next.
hello world, octo says hi to everyone who trains it.
the brain of the assistant can answer, remember, and now also imagine.
train octo on any text file and it will learn that language too.
bangla text is supported: অক্টো একটি ছোট ভাষা মডেল, যা অফলাইনে চলে।
সে প্রতিদিন নতুন কথা শেখে এবং ধীরে ধীরে বড় হয়।
weather is nice today, the assistant can check the news and the clock.
octo runs fully offline, no cloud and no paid api is required.
small models are slow to learn but they are honest and simple.
